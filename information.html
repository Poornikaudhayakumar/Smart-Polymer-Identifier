Polymer Information

Learn about the polymers included in this identification system.

Polyethylene

Classification: Synthetic thermoplastic

Polymerization: Addition polymerization

Properties: Lightweight, flexible and chemically resistant.

Structure:

–[CH₂–CH₂]ₙ–

Applications: Packaging, containers and pipes.

Nylon 6,6

Classification: Synthetic thermoplastic

Polymerization: Condensation polymerization

Properties: Strong, durable and resistant to wear.

Structure:

–[NH–(CH₂)₆–NH–CO–(CH₂)₄–CO]ₙ–

Applications: Fibres, textiles and engineering components.

Epoxy Resin

Classification: Synthetic thermosetting polymer

Polymerization: Cross-linking / curing

Properties: Hard, strong and chemically resistant.

Structure:

Cross-linked three-dimensional network

Applications: Adhesives, coatings and electronic components.
